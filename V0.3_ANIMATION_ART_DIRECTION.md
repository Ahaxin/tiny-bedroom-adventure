# V0.3 — Storybook Animation / 绘本动画升级

## Goal / 目标
Keep the existing five playable levels and their puzzle logic, but make the scene feel like a handcrafted, animated miniature of the photographed bedroom rather than a collection of unlit primitives.

## Visual anchors from the private reference photo
- Grey pleated curtains and daylight entering from the **left**.
- White desk, dark laptop, external monitor and shelf at the **left back**.
- Black mesh swivel chair in front of the desk.
- Broad white headboard and cream bedding at the **right**.
- Loose white/blue duvet and pink blanket on the bed.
- Warm timber floor, narrow standing lamp on the far right.
- A fluffy cream colourpoint cat with darker face/ears, blue eyes, lying on the bed.

## Implementation
1. Stylized lighting: warm/cool directional lighting, quantized shadows, soft rim light, material-aware procedural fabric, wood, fur and screen shading. No third-party runtime dependencies.
2. Character animation: articulated walking, foot roll, torso counter-rotation, head bob, scarf sway and landing compression. Keep input and collision untouched.
3. Cat animation: breathing, tail motion, ears, paws, whiskers and natural blink cycle; keep all puzzle trigger positions.
4. Environmental animation: gently shifting curtains, daylight pools, floating dust motes and warm vignette.
5. Environment detail: soft fabric layers, stitched pillow edges, bed linen folds, desk accents, cat whiskers and fur tufts.
6. Performance: mobile pixel-ratio cap, animation reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`, Canvas fallback must remain functional.
7. Verification: existing logic tests, new art-motion checks, static HTML build, desktop and mobile smoke tests.

## Acceptance
- At first glance: same left desk/right bed arrangement and same colourpoint cat.
- Walk cycle has clear alternating legs/arms, grounded foot motion, visible body weight and recovery on landing.
- Cat breathes and blinks even when idle; fur and tail show subtle secondary motion.
- Room has visible lighting, textured materials and richer silhouette, not only flat boxes.
- Chinese/Dutch UI, five levels, checkpoints, mobile touch controls and GitHub Pages remain intact.

## Limits
Procedural art is a visual improvement, not a photogrammetric reconstruction. No source photo or personal portrait is published. Full professional character animation and photoreal cat fur would require dedicated 3D assets and rigging.
